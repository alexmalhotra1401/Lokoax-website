Content quality

Quality is a process, not a badge.

LOKOAX content should be accurate, understandable, curriculum-aware and genuinely useful in practice.

1

Map the curriculum

Organize subjects into clear topics, chapters and learning outcomes.

2

Build for understanding

Write explanations, examples and visuals around the learner’s point of view.

3

Design purposeful practice

Connect questions and quizzes directly to the concepts being learned.

4

Review and improve

Use educator review, learner feedback and product data to keep content useful.
