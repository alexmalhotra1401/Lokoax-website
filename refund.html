Refund / Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how Premium cancellation, renewal, billing issues and potential refunds can be handled.

Last updated: Sep 2026

Quick summary

✓Your account and learning activity may contain personal data.
✓Payments should be handled by secure payment providers.
✓You should have clear privacy and subscription choices.
✓Material changes should be communicated transparently.

01Cancelling a subscription

Users should be able to cancel Premium from the account or billing portal.

  • Cancellation can stop future renewals while allowing access through the already-paid billing period.

02When cancellation takes effect

Unless required otherwise by law or stated at checkout, cancellation should normally take effect at the end of the current paid billing period.

03Refund eligibility

Refund rules must align with the payment provider, applicable consumer law and the commercial policy adopted by LOKOAX.

  • Define whether monthly subscriptions are generally non-refundable after access begins.
  • Define any cooling-off, duplicate-charge or technical-failure exceptions.

04Annual plans

If annual subscriptions are offered, clearly state whether partial refunds are available and how any statutory cancellation rights apply.

05Duplicate or incorrect charges

Users should contact support promptly for duplicate charges, billing errors or payments they do not recognize.

  • Payment-provider investigation steps may be required before a refund is issued.

06Failed renewals

When renewal fails, LOKOAX may retry payment, request an updated payment method or provide a short grace period before Premium access changes.

07Access after cancellation

After Premium ends, the account can return to available Free access while retaining saved progress and account history where practical.

08How to request help

Provide the account email, transaction details and a brief description of the issue when contacting billing support. Avoid sending full card information by email.

09Processing time

Approved refunds may take several business days to appear, depending on the original payment method and payment provider.

10Policy updates & contact

Add the official billing contact, legal entity, jurisdiction-specific rights and final refund windows before publication.

Need help with a subscription?

Contact LOKOAX support for product and account questions. Legal notices and formal contact details can be added here before launch.
