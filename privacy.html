Privacy Policy

This page explains how LOKOAX can collect, use, protect and manage personal information across the website and learning platform.

01Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly and information generated while you use LOKOAX.

  • Account details such as name, email and profile information.
  • Learning activity such as lessons completed, quiz results and progress.
  • Technical information such as browser, device and usage events.

02How we use information

Information can be used to operate, personalize, secure and improve the learning experience.

  • Provide account access and save progress.
  • Process subscriptions and support requests.
  • Understand product performance and improve content.

03Payments

Payment processing should be handled by approved payment providers; LOKOAX should avoid storing full payment-card details where possible.

  • Billing records may include plan, amount, payment status and renewal information.

04Cookies & analytics

Cookies and similar technologies may support sign-in, preferences, analytics and security.

  • A separate Cookie Policy can explain categories and consent options.

05Sharing & service providers

Data may be shared only with vendors needed to operate the service, subject to appropriate contractual safeguards.

  • Examples can include hosting, authentication, email, analytics and payments.

06Data retention & security

Retain information only as long as needed for product, legal and operational purposes, with reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.

07Your choices & rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct or delete information and manage communication preferences.

08Children & student privacy

If LOKOAX serves minors, age requirements, parental consent and school-related privacy obligations should be defined clearly before launch.

09Policy updates & contact

Material changes should be communicated appropriately. Add the official privacy contact and legal entity details before publication.

Questions about this policy?

Contact LOKOAX support for product and account questions. Legal notices and formal contact details can be added here before launch.
